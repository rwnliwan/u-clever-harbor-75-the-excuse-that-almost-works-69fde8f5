## The entry

> Sorry, the bin lorry blocked my drive.

38 characters, 8 words — one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on
GTA 6 launch week. The judge is a tired manager who has heard every excuse
twice; the angle that wins is a real, specific, boring workplace detail
rather than a wild story. A bin lorry blocking the drive is mundane,
concrete, and impossible to argue with from the office — no setup, no
explanation, no signature, a text sent in a hurry.
